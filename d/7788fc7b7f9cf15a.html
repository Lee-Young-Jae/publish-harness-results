A Morning Run Through the City RUNNING Seoul Tokyo New York London
Many travelers like to keep up their usual routines, even on trips.
This time, start your morning with a run.
You'll catch sides of the city you'd never
see the day before, and kick off the day with energy.
A Day Walking Among Autumn Leaves HIKING Seoraksan Kyoto Quebec
Fall foliage is a luxury only this season offers.
Walk along paths lined with red-tinted
trees and lose yourself in the beauty.
Layering thin outerwear is smart here
— it helps you handle the temperature swing
between the base and higher elevations.
Wellness, Among Nature YOGA Bali Phuket Hawaii
Why not train body and mind surrounded by lush nature?
Breathing deeply while sensing life
beyond your own offers the ultimate healing.
You don't need to be an expert. Puppy yoga, where you
connect with dogs, draws reviews calling it 'heavenly.'
Surrender to a Southeast Asian yoga class,
where even a passing lizard feels charming.
Cool Breezes and the Green GOLF Kagoshima Bangkok Nha Trang
Perfect weather for golf — especially in Southeast Asia,
once the rainy season ends and clear skies return.
The appeal is pairing golf with resort relaxation.
Since it's cool out, mornings can be chilly while afternoons warm up,
so pack a windbreaker and neck warmer to stay comfortable.
